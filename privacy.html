Sunplot

Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Published by [Publisher name]

The short version. Sunplot has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your plans stay on your phone. When you search an address or look at a property, the app asks public map and data services for that spot's information. Property details, including owner names, come from public county records.

What stays on your device

The places you save, your property boundaries, beds, trees, buildings, crops and notes are stored only in the app on your device. We don't run a server that receives or keeps them. Deleting a place in the app, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Your location

Sunplot uses your device's location only when you tap the location button, to center the map on where you are and look up the property there. You can say no; everything else in the app still works. We don't store your location or send it to us.

What the app sends to outside services

To answer what you ask for, the app sends the address you type, or the map position you tap or view, straight from your device to these public services. Each one sees your device's internet address, as any website does, and has its own privacy policy.

  • Address search: Esri World Geocoder, with OpenStreetMap Nominatim as a backup
  • Property lines and records: state and county map services (for example NC OneMap), and ArcGIS Online to find county maps
  • County, district and census information: US Census Bureau
  • Aerial photos, topo maps, elevation and streams: US Geological Survey; aerial photos also from state agencies
  • Soil and farm photos: US Department of Agriculture
  • Flood maps: FEMA. Wetlands: US Fish and Wildlife Service
  • Frost records: NOAA regional climate centers (ACIS). Forecasts: National Weather Service
  • Fonts: Google Fonts. Hosting: GitHub Pages

We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising or any other purpose, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Public property records

Property lines and the details shown for a parcel, which can include owner names, mailing addresses, values and sale dates, are public records published by state and county governments. Sunplot displays them as published; it doesn't add to or change them. They can be out of date or wrong, and they are not a survey or a legal record.

Ask us to hide owner details. If you own a property and don't want your name and mailing address shown in Sunplot, email [your support email] with the property address. We'll hide those fields for that property in the next app update, usually within 30 days. To remove the information from the public record itself, contact your county; many states have programs that shield the addresses of people such as survivors of domestic violence, judges and law enforcement officers.

Children

Sunplot is meant for adults and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.
