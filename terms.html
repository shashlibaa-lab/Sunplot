Property lines and public records are not a survey

Property lines and parcel details come from state and county tax maps and other public map services. Tax maps are drawn for tax purposes. They are often off by many feet, can be years out of date, and don't show easements, setbacks, rights of way or recorded boundary changes. Sunplot shows this information as the government publishes it and doesn't check or correct it.

Don't rely on Sunplot to decide where a property line, fence, building, well, septic system or driveway goes, or to settle a boundary question. Use a licensed surveyor and your county's official records for that.

Estimates, not guarantees

Sun hours, shade, slope, soil, frost dates, flood zones, wetlands, planting dates and seed counts are estimates from public data and math. Tree and building heights are your guesses. Shade from hills isn't counted. Weather and growing results vary from year to year. Sunplot doesn't give legal, survey, engineering, insurance, real estate or financial advice. For flood insurance or building permits, use FEMA's official maps and your local permit office.
